## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues, pull requests or discussions.** Making them public before they are fixed can put users at risk.

Instead, contact the development team by email at **diakonia.labs@gmail.com**, using the subject `[SECURITY] Short description`.

## What to include in your report

- **Platform:** the affected component (web, API or app) and the operating system, device or browser where it happens.
- **Version:** the app version, release tag or commit hash.
- **Steps to reproduce:** a numbered list, including any required configuration.
- **Additional information:** expected and actual behavior, potential impact, screenshots, logs, proof of concept or a suggested fix.

Please remove passwords, access tokens and real personal data from anything you attach.

## What happens next

We will review your report and reply to the email address you contacted us from. Please keep the details private until the issue has been fixed or we have agreed on a disclosure date with you.

Thank you for helping keep Diakonia-Labs and its users safe.
